About Me

Hi! I'm Bryan Robin M. Santiago, an entry-level Game Developer passionate about creating engaging and interactive gameplay experiences. I have experience developing games in Unity, working on both 2D and 3D projects, with a focus on gameplay programming, user interface implementation, and game mechanics.

Through my academic projects and internship experience, I have built a solid foundation in game development, gameplay testing, bug reporting, and collaborative development workflows. I enjoy solving technical challenges, learning new technologies, and working with multidisciplinary teams to bring game ideas to life.

In addition to Unity development, I have a basic understanding of Unreal Engine, basic knowledge of UI/UX design, and a basic understanding of 3D modeling. I also have experience in sound design, allowing me to contribute across different aspects of the game development pipeline. I am always eager to expand my skills, learn from experienced developers, and continuously improve as a game developer.

My goal is to grow as a professional game developer while contributing to meaningful and enjoyable gaming experiences. I am excited to take on new challenges, collaborate with talented teams, and continue learning throughout my career in the game industry.
