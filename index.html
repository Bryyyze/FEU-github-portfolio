About Me

Hi! I'm Bryan Robin M. Santiago, an entry-level Game Developer with a passion for creating engaging and immersive gameplay experiences. I have experience developing games in Unity, working on both 2D and 3D projects, with a focus on gameplay programming, user interface implementation, and interactive game mechanics.

Through my academic projects and internship experience, I have developed a strong foundation in game development, gameplay testing, bug reporting, and collaborative development workflows. I enjoy solving technical challenges, learning new tools, and working closely with artists and designers to transform ideas into polished gameplay experiences.

In addition to programming, I have experience with sound design and a basic understanding of 3D modeling, allowing me to contribute across multiple areas of game development. I am always eager to expand my skills, learn from experienced developers, and stay up to date with industry best practices.

As an entry-level developer, my goal is to contribute to a passionate team, continue growing as a programmer, and help create memorable games that players enjoy. I am committed to continuous learning and excited to take on new challenges while building high-quality gaming experiences.
